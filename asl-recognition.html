How to Use

  1. Allow camera access when prompted by your browser
  2. Position your hand clearly in front of the camera
  3. Make ASL alphabet signs (A-Z)
  4. The predicted letter will appear on the left with confidence percentage

Setup Your Own Model

This application requires a trained model from Teachable Machine.

Train your model with ASL alphabet signs (A-Z), export it, and replace the modelURL in the source code with your model's URL.
